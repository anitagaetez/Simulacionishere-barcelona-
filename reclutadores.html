What is isHere?

An app that helps tourists find what they need, nearby and right now: sunscreen, a charger, an umbrella, home cooking… They say what they're looking for and the app takes them to the local shop that has it.

What does the role involve?

  • You visit shops in Barcelona and introduce isHere.
  • The shop signs up with your personal code.
  • For each sign-up you earn €5 + VAT, paid instantly and automatically.
  • No schedule, no minimums, no exclusivity.
  • We give you materials, your personal link and support.

Illustrative example: if 20 shops sign up with your code in a month, you earn €100 + VAT. Results depend on your effort.

We're looking for people who

  • Enjoy talking to people.
  • Are persistent: a "no" doesn't put them off.
  • Know Barcelona's neighbourhoods.
  • Speak Spanish or Catalan fluently (English is a plus).
  • Are over 18 and registered as self-employed in Spain (or willing to register).

Independent commercial collaboration, not an employment contract.
